Finding 1 · Statutory & EC Bottlenecks ₹218 Cr Impact

Rigs Mobilized to Wells Lacking MoEFCC EC or Defence NOC

Rigs finished drilling a well (`WELL_COMPLETED`) and were towed to the geographically closest location—only to sit idle for weeks because the target well lacked Environmental Clearance (`EC`), DRDO/Navy `NOC`, or a `500m` subsea pipeline buffer.

Finding 2 · Monsoon & MWS Sea-State Mismatch ₹184 Cr Impact

Missing Calm MWS Windows (`Hs ≤ 1.50m`) & Storm Riser Damage Risk

Delayed tug coordination caused completed rigs to miss 38-hour calm sea windows (`Hs ≤ 1.50m`), trapping them on location when swells built above `2.5m`, while deepwater drillships lacked automated 48h LMRP disconnect & helicopter evacuation triggers.

Finding 3 · Fragmented Fleet & Tug Dispatch ₹110 Cr Impact

Manual Spreadsheet Planning Across 20 Rigs & 120 Candidate Wells

Well-to-well sequence decisions were made in isolated silos without real-time ECMWF/Open-Meteo wave feeds or Monte Carlo wet-tow simulations (`3× AHTS 150T Bollard Pull Tugs`).
